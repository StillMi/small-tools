Add the companion portal stone and fix the bugs a full audit turned up

The companion stone asks the target player, waits ten seconds and is spent
only once the sender has actually landed next to them.

Six read-only auditors went through the mod block by block; every finding
was re-checked against the source before it was fixed.

Companion stone:
- findLanding required standable(pos) and standable(pos.above()); the first
  already proves pos is air, which can never hold up the block above it, so
  the pair was unsatisfiable and the stone had never once teleported anyone
- the panel was drawn after super.render and buried the accept/reject buttons
- accepting could start a second countdown on top of a running one
- the stone was spent even when the teleport was cancelled by another mod
- a request kept hanging until timeout when either side logged out

Portal runtime:
- old saves migrate to a SHARED_ACTOR creator, but the quota tables only
  matched actor.equals(creator), so every player got a fresh quota and the
  pair never expired
- the structure self-check only ran on the master block, and the onRemove
  fallback is skipped while building, so a master overwritten by another
  portal left the rest of the frame teleporting to a stale partner
- configured was cleared before an invalidate that can no-op, leaving a
  portal that still stands but can no longer be used or removed
- a partner with no reachable block entity still teleported the entity
- block placement in a portal frame was swallowed by a re-entrant write

Portal building:
- the doorway clearing loop skipped block entities while the placement loop
  overwrote the same cells, deleting chests and spawners together with their
  contents
- closing a previous pair destroyed both ends whenever a single one stood,
  flattening whatever the player had rebuilt on the other side
- sites were chosen before the old pair was taken down, so rebuilding in
  place always failed with NO_GROUND
- nothing checked whether a player occupied the far end

Wash stone:
- the locked check ran after the lists were cleared, so resizing the window
  wiped the candidates, stranded the input slot and kept the stone
- the stone was charged when an entry was picked, not when the swap landed
- the list was not rebuilt after a swap because the edit bypassed setChanged
- candidates ignored exclusive_set and could roll Sharpness with Smite

Enhancer:
- invested xp was stranded forever once the item could no longer be worked on
- tryFill ignored exclusive_set and treasure enchantments
- the client kept its own copy of the button rules while canEnhance had no
  callers at all
- the menu showed nine hotbar slots but counted all thirty-six

Tooling:
- deploy.py deleted every installed jar before copying, so a locked jar left
  the mods directory empty; it now copies to a temp file, verifies and does an
  atomic replace, and only prunes versions older than the one being deployed
- MagicButton fed raw milliseconds into Mth.sin, which saturates the int and
  froze the hover outline
